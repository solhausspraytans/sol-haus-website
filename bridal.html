Preparation

Bridal prep notes.

  • Exfoliate the day before your tan, and finish shaving or waxing in advance.
  • Arrive clean — no lotion, deodorant, perfume or makeup on the skin being tanned.
  • Plan your treatments — schedule nails, facials and waxing before your tan where possible.
  • Wear loose, dark clothing and bring something comfortable to change into.
