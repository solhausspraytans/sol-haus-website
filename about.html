Meet the glow behind Sol Haus

I'm Katie, the hands (and heart) behind every Sol Haus tan.

It all started in college. The day I earned my first spray tan certification, the kitchen of our sorority house became my very first studio. Every tan was another excuse to hang out, catch up and glow together.

Tanning was never new to me, though. I grew up as a competitive Irish dancer, where a “body-builder, stage-ready tan” was part of the uniform, and I was sprayed before every competition. I realized this could go far beyond just my friends, and it completely changed everything.

I graduated from university with a dual master’s degree in Criminal Justice and Cyber Security, while also earning my master’s in spray tanning. Interesting mix?

From bachelorettes, vacations, sports photos, proms and sip-and-tans to competitions all over the world, I’ve been able to do it all. Being mobile is the reason I’ve met so many people and been in so many different places.

My favorite thing about spray tans is the vibe of the room and meeting all of my new clients. We may never have crossed paths before, but now I get to bring confidence to everyone through one appointment.
