Competition prep

Before you dance.

  • Exfoliate the day before and arrive with clean, product-free skin.
  • Wear dark, loose clothing to and from your appointment.
  • Keep costumes and wigs away from freshly tanned skin until you've rinsed.
  • Ask about timing — we'll plan your appointment around your competition day.
