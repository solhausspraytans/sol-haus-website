The fine print

Policies

Cancellations & no-shows

Every booking reserves a spot that could have been given to another client. If you don’t show up for your appointment, or don’t let me know at least 24 hours in advance, a $25 fee will be charged.

Late arrivals

Communication is key — if you’re running late, please let me know as soon as possible. If you arrive more than 20 minutes late without contacting me, I may need to reschedule your appointment, and the $25 late/no-show fee will apply.

Refunds & touch-ups

Spray tans are non-refundable. If your results don’t match the shade we agreed on, and all prep and aftercare instructions were followed, I’ll offer one complimentary touch-up within 48 hours.

Your shade & results

We’ll discuss and agree on your desired shade before your session, and any concerns can be worked through during the session for a perfect final result. How long your tan lasts depends on following your prep and aftercare.

Group appointments

Please be respectful of other clients, competitors and the overall session atmosphere.

Travel

Sol Haus is fully mobile. Depending on your location, a travel fee may apply and will be confirmed with you before your appointment is booked.

By booking with Sol Haus, you acknowledge and accept these terms. Read and sign the full client agreement.

Privacy policy

Sol Haus Spray Tans collects the information you choose to share through our inquiry forms (such as your name, contact details and event information) only to respond to your inquiry and arrange your appointment. We do not sell your information.

Inquiry forms are processed by a third-party form service, and booking is handled by our external booking provider; each has its own privacy policy. Health and consent information is collected only through our secure external consent form, never stored on this website.

If you'd like us to delete information you've sent us, please email hangtanspray@gmail.com.
